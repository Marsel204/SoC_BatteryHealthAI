Processing

Each discharge is paired with its preceding charge and nearest impedance sweep. Rules extract nine features; SoH = measured capacity ÷ 2 Ah × 100.

Quality: {{n_flagged}} cycles lack a normal charge curve. Their four charging features stay blank in the analysis. Only charging features are known at charge end.

Raw files and cycle alignment

The raw files contain {{n_ops}} timed operations. Skipping each cell’s first discharge leaves {{n_cycles}} aligned cycles. Source checksums are verified before processing.

{{eis_after_charge}} sweeps start after charging ends. Earlier starts also do not confirm completion, because sweep end times are unavailable.

Flagged cycles and data checks

Three flagged records begin with implausible voltage spikes; two B0018 records resemble top-up charges. None has samples in the normal charging climb.

Analysis

Main finding: charge duration and the charge-curve peak track health most closely. Their information overlaps. Correlation describes association, not prediction accuracy or cause.

Models

Compare Ridge, ElasticNet, SVR, GPR and XGBoost. Leave-one-battery-out evaluation trains on three cells and tests on the fourth, repeated for every cell.

Read the scores as development estimates. All four cells were explored. Some inputs need discharge or later impedance data, and the training table contains interpolated charging values. These results do not establish charge-end prediction or performance on a new cell.

For the live simulator, run the combined app. Model definitions and evaluation notes are in Models.
