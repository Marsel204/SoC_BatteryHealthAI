Physics-Informed Battery State of Health (SoH) Estimation

A 4-stage prognostics methodology combining empirical time-series telemetry from NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE) 18650 Li-ion cells with equivalent circuit models (ECM), irreversible thermodynamics, and epistemic Bayesian uncertainty quantification.

Target Battery Cells: B0005, B0006, B0007, B0018
Ambient Temperature: 24°C Constant
EoL Failure Criterion: 70% SoH (1.40 Ah)
1. Four-Stage Physics-Informed Pipeline End-to-End Workflow Architecture
Stage 1 · Data Parsing
Operation Alignment & Quirks Handling

NASA .mat files log charge, discharge, and impedance sequentially without pre-grouped cycle tags. Our parser temporally aligns preceding charge CC/CV curves and nearest EIS sweeps (\(R_e, R_{ct}\)) to each discharge capacity label.

✓ Drops Cycle 1 Anomaly
✓ Computes Lithium Diffusion rest_time_hours
Stage 2 · Physics Features
Feature Extraction & dQ/dV Denoising

Extracts 9 observable channels: \(t_{\text{cc}}\), \(t_{\text{cv}}\), \((dQ/dV)_{\text{peak}}\), \(V_{\text{peak}}\), \(\Delta T_{\text{max}}\), \(\Delta V_{\text{ohmic}}\), \(R_e\), \(R_{ct}\), and \(t_{\text{rest}}\). Employs Savitzky-Golay polynomial filtering (\(N=9\), order 2) on uniform 10 mV voltage grids to eliminate numerical differentiation noise spikes.

✓ Uniform Binning \(\Delta V = 10\text{ mV}\)
✓ Derivative Stability at Cutoff Thresholds
Stage 3 · Validation Protocol
Leave-One-Battery-Out (LOBO)

Standard random train/test splits cause severe optimistic bias due to temporal autocorrelation within the same battery. We enforce strict LOBO cross-validation: training on 3 cells (e.g. \(B0005, B0006, B0007\)) and evaluating on the fourth unseen cell (\(B0018\)).

✓ Zero Time-Series Leakage
✓ Validates Cell-to-Cell Transferability
Stage 4 · Physics Grounding
Electrochemical Proofs & Audits

Models are audited against first-principles battery physics. Empirical weights and Jacobian sensitivities \(\mathcal{J}_i = \partial \widehat{\text{SoH}}/\partial x_i\) must satisfy the thermodynamic and kinetic constraints of the Thevenin Terminal Voltage equation and Joule Heating dissipation.

✓ Thevenin Overpotential Proof
✓ Joule Heating Law Thermodynamic Sign

3. Incremental Capacity (dQ/dV) Degradation Anatomy Electrochemical Phase Transitions & Mode Isolation
Highlight Mode:
Graphite Staging & Redox Phase Peaks
The Incremental Capacity curve converts plateau regions in the charging curve into distinct diagnostic peaks. Peak 1 at \(\approx 3.90\text{ V}\) reflects Stage 2 graphite intercalation (\(\text{LiC}_{12} \to \text{LiC}_6\)), while Peak 2 at \(\approx 4.02\text{ V}\) reflects cathode transition-metal redox couples.
Mathematical Formulation
$$\frac{dQ}{dV} = \frac{I(t)}{dV/dt}, \quad \Delta V_{\text{peak}} \propto I \cdot (R_e + R_{ct})$$

Why GPR Outperforms Deterministic Models

Deterministic regressors provide single-point estimates without awareness of unseen feature regimes. GPR computes the exact analytical epistemic posterior:

$$\mu_* = \mathbf{k}_*^T (\mathbf{K} + \sigma_n^2 \mathbf{I})^{-1}\mathbf{y}, \quad \sigma_*^2 = k_* - \mathbf{k}_*^T (\mathbf{K} + \sigma_n^2 \mathbf{I})^{-1}\mathbf{k}_*$$

Notice how the confidence ribbon pinches tight near observed training cycles and widens significantly into degradation regimes with fewer samples.

Analytical Risk: P(SoH ≤ 70%) = Φ((70 - μ) / σ)
